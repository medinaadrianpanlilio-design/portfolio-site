# Product

## What this is

A personal landing page and project-first portfolio for Adrian Alexander P. Medina, a fourth-year Computer Science student at Pampanga State University. The audience is software-engineering internship recruiters and hiring managers.

## Mode

**Landing page first, work close behind.** The visitor should meet Adrian, his skills, and his stack immediately, then move naturally into the systems he has shipped. A grounded AI guide can answer questions about the approved portfolio.

## Ten-second takeaway

“This is a Computer Science student who can build backend, web, and mobile systems—and has real POS/admin, API, and mobile work to show for it.”

## Success

A recruiter can:

- understand Adrian’s positioning, focus, and availability in the first view;
- see the skills and stack he works with;
- ask the AI guide about the work and get an answer grounded in the portfolio;
- open **Magilas Gym**, **HealthyaSat**, and **Iahbrary** from the landing page or the full project index;
- understand each project’s role, stack, problem, and engineering decisions;
- open the live products, the public Iahbrary and SkySprout source, and Adrian’s LinkedIn profile;
- find a deeper skills and education summary on About;
- reach a clearly secondary SkySprout hobby note at the end of the project index.

The site must be fast, readable on a phone, and visually specific without becoming a template.

## Non-goals

- A 3D game experience or game-first hero.
- A centralized “game” narrative that outranks the professional work.
- A CMS, auth, blog, contact form, or account system. The AI guide endpoint is the only server-side component.
- Invented retention metrics, testimonials, player counts, or client claims.
- Publishing private repository data, a phone number, a street address, or the outdated résumé.
- Copying the reference deck’s template or imagery.
