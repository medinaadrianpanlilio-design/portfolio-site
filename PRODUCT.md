# Product

## What this is

A one-page portfolio for Adrian Alexander P. Medina, a fourth-year Computer Science student at Pampanga State University. The audience is software-engineering internship recruiters and hiring managers.

## Mode

**Experience.** The work leads; the interface recedes. The visitor should feel that a person with a point of view made this, not that a template was filled in.

## Ten-second takeaway

“This person made a real game for the love of it, it is live, and they can reason about the systems underneath it.”

## Success

A recruiter understands who Adrian is, sees SkySprout as a live shipped product, understands its loop and retention design, can inspect Iahbrary’s public code, sees the range across HealthyaSat and Magilas Gym, and can contact him without friction.

The site itself should be fast, readable on a phone, and visually specific.

## Non-goals

- A playable 3D game.
- A backend, CMS, auth, blog, or contact form.
- Invented retention metrics, testimonials, or player counts.
- Publishing the SkySprout source, private repository data, a phone number, a street address, or the outdated résumé.
- Copying the reference deck’s template or imagery.
